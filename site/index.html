    How SpillWatch AI works

    Storm overflows are allowed to release untreated sewage only during heavy rain. Since 2024 every water company in England publishes when each of its ~14,000 overflows is spilling. SpillWatch AI collects that data every 15 minutes and adds two AI models.

    1. Unexpected spills

    A model trained on ~1.8 million past spills learns how likely each overflow is to spill given the rainfall nearby (on the day, the previous day and over the past 30 days) and how much rain that overflow normally needs. A spill the model considered very unlikely for the weather is flagged as unexpected.

    Limitations

    • An unexpected spill is not proof of illegal activity. Monitors can fail, and local rain can be missed by gauges up to 10 km away.
    • Anglian, South West, Southern and Thames Water do not publish event history, so their overflows are scored without site history (lower confidence).
    • Forecasts are probabilities; about half of high-risk warnings do not end in a spill.
    • About 7% of overflows have no rain gauge within 10 km and are not scored.
